# PRISM Clinical Evidence Contract v0

## Purpose

This contract defines the shared representation of a clinical fact that can move between PRISM intake, ASR/NLU, OCR/document intelligence, ABDM/FHIR adapters, allopathic and AYUSH pathways, and MedBridge reconciliation.

The contract is intentionally **evidence-first and resource-grounded**. A downstream service must be able to answer:

1. What was said, observed or extracted?
2. Which clinical pathway does it belong to?
3. Where did it come from?
4. Which exact resource supports it?
5. How was it captured?
6. What verification/trust state does it have?
7. What was it normalized to, if normalization succeeded?
8. How confident is the system in the capture?

## Required semantics

Every evidence item has:

- `id` — stable local identifier.
- `kind` — clinical concept type.
- `clinicalPathway` — `allopathic`, `ayush`, `cross_cutting` or `unknown`.
- `source` — broad origin: `patient`, `document`, `fhir`, `clinician` or `system`.
- `sourceClass` — trust/origin class: patient-side, patient-provided document, ABDM exchange, clinician-confirmed, system-derived or unknown.
- `captureMethod` — voice, touch, OCR, FHIR, manual or derived.
- `value` — structured value consumed by downstream services.
- `resourceRefs` — one or more exact source resources supporting the evidence.
- `verification` — how the evidence entered the system and what has actually been verified.
- `provenance` — source identity and event/ingestion context.

## Clinical pathway is first-class

SETU must not flatten all clinical information into one allopathic-only model.

Use:

- `allopathic` — conventional clinical-history concepts and care records.
- `ayush` — AYUSH-specific history and assessment concepts.
- `cross_cutting` — identity, demographics, documents, allergies, medications or other information legitimately shared across pathways.
- `unknown` — pathway not yet determined.

A cross-cutting fact must not be duplicated merely to make it fit both pathways. Pathway classification describes clinical context, not the value of the fact.

## Source classes

`sourceClass` distinguishes where a fact entered SETU:

- `patient_reported` — directly stated or selected by the patient through voice or touch.
- `patient_provided_document` — extracted from a document supplied during intake.
- `abdm_exchange` — imported through an appropriately consented ABDM/ABHA health-information exchange.
- `clinician_confirmed` — explicitly reviewed/confirmed by an authorized clinician.
- `system_derived` — generated by deterministic processing or bounded AI from existing evidence.
- `unknown` — origin cannot yet be classified.

These classes must never be silently collapsed.

## ABHA/ABDM does not mean clinically true

An `abdm_exchange` item means the information was obtained through the applicable consented interoperability path. It does **not** automatically mean:

- the clinical fact is current,
- the fact is correct,
- the patient agrees with it today, or
- a clinician has confirmed it during the present encounter.

Identity assurance, transport provenance and clinical truth are different concepts.

## Resource references are mandatory

Every evidence item must have at least one `resourceRef`.

Examples:

### Patient voice

```text
ConversationTurn/intake-session-001/turn-07
```

### Patient touch

```text
TouchResponse/intake-session-001/question-allergy
```

### OCR document

```text
Document/document-042
page=2
region=bbox
```

### ABDM/FHIR

```text
Observation/observation-987
FHIR resource reference preserved
```

### Clinician confirmation

```text
ClinicianReview/review-011
```

A derived finding may reference multiple upstream resources.

## Provenance is mandatory

Clinical evidence must never be detached from its origin.

For example, these remain distinct even if their normalized concept is identical:

1. A patient reports an allergy during PRISM voice intake.
2. A discharge summary contains an allergy extracted by OCR.
3. An ABDM/FHIR record contains an allergy resource.
4. A clinician confirms the allergy.

MedBridge may reconcile them later, but the original evidence boundaries remain intact.

## Original vs normalized value

`originalValue` preserves the source representation. `value` is the structured value used by the pipeline. `normalized` records a terminology mapping when established.

A failed or uncertain normalization must not overwrite the original evidence.

## Confidence vs clinical verification

These are deliberately separate:

- `confidence` answers: **How confident is the system that it captured/extracted this correctly?**
- `verification.state` answers: **What kind of verification or trust event has actually occurred?**

Neither field alone means the underlying clinical claim is true.

## Dates

- `provenance.eventDate` is when the clinical event/evidence refers to.
- `provenance.ingestedAt` is when SETU received the evidence.

They must not be conflated.

## Derived evidence

System-derived evidence must:

- identify itself as `system_derived`,
- retain all material upstream `resourceRefs`,
- never replace the underlying source evidence,
- preserve uncertainty, and
- remain distinguishable from patient statements and clinician confirmations.

## Safety boundary

Evidence is not a diagnosis and is not a clinical decision. PRISM records and structures information; safety gates may escalate red flags; downstream services may surface conflicts. Final clinical decisions remain with the clinician.

## Compatibility rule

Changes to required fields, enums, pathway semantics, source classes, verification semantics or resource-reference semantics are breaking changes and require a new schema version or an explicitly reviewed compatibility decision.
