# MedBridge

MedBridge is SETU's longitudinal clinical evidence and reconciliation layer.

Responsibilities:

- aggregate permitted clinical evidence from PRISM, documents and interoperable sources
- normalize and compare evidence across time and sources
- detect conflicts, duplicates and clinically relevant gaps
- generate source-cited, evidence-grounded synthesis
- support clinician-facing clinical queries over permitted evidence

MedBridge does not make the final clinical decision. Physician review remains part of the workflow.
