# Allopathic Clinical History

Defines the structured clinical-history sections and rules for the allopathic pathway.

Initial sections:

- presenting complaint
- history of present illness
- past medical history
- past surgical/procedure history
- medication history
- allergies/adverse reactions
- family history
- personal/social history where relevant
- review of systems
- prior investigations
- current concerns

The question engine must branch from clinical context while preserving patient wording and uncertainty.
