# AYUSH Clinical History

AYUSH is a first-class clinical pathway in SETU.

The PS specifically calls out the additional depth of Ayurvedic history-taking. The implementation should support practitioner-reviewed, configurable pathways rather than infer Ayurvedic findings from an allopathic questionnaire.

Initial Ayurveda concepts include, as applicable to the clinical protocol:

- Prakriti
- Vikriti
- Agni
- Koshtha
- Ahara-Vihara
- Nidana
- Samprapti
- Dashavidha Pariksha dimensions identified by the PS

The pathway must remain extensible to additional AYUSH systems and institutional protocols.
